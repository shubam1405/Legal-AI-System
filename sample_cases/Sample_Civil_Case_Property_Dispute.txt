================================================================================
                         FACTUAL BACKGROUND
================================================================================

CHRONOLOGY OF EVENTS:

1. ORIGINAL OWNERSHIP (1995):
   - The subject property was originally owned by Late Shri Mohan Lal Sharma
     (father of the Plaintiff) who purchased it vide Sale Deed dated 
     12th March 1995, registered as Document No. 4567/1995 at Sub-Registrar 
     Office, Gurugram.

2. INHERITANCE (2010):
   - Late Shri Mohan Lal Sharma passed away on 5th June 2010.
   - The Plaintiff, being the only legal heir (as per the registered Will 
     dated 15th January 2008), inherited the subject property.
   - Mutation was duly recorded in revenue records on 20th August 2010.

3. POWER OF ATTORNEY (2018):
   - On 10th January 2018, the Plaintiff executed a General Power of Attorney 
     (GPA) in favor of Defendant No. 1 for the limited purpose of:
     a) Obtaining necessary approvals from municipal authorities
     b) Getting the property demarcated
     c) Applying for change of land use (CLU)
   - The GPA was registered as Document No. 789/2018.
   - The GPA specifically stated it was NOT for sale or transfer of property.

4. FRAUDULENT TRANSACTION (2020):
   - On 15th March 2020, without the knowledge or consent of the Plaintiff,
     Defendant No. 1, misusing the GPA, executed a Sale Deed in favor of 
     Defendant No. 2 (his own company) for a consideration of Rs. 50,00,000/-
     (grossly undervalued).
   - The Sale Deed was registered as Document No. 2345/2020.

5. DISCOVERY OF FRAUD (2024):
   - In September 2024, when the Plaintiff attempted to sell the property,
     he discovered that the property had already been transferred.
   - The Plaintiff immediately filed a police complaint (FIR No. 567/2024)
     at PS Sector 14, Gurugram on 25th September 2024 under Sections 
     420 (Cheating), 467 (Forgery), 468 (Forgery for cheating), and 
     471 (Using forged document) of IPC.

6. NOTICE AND FAILED NEGOTIATIONS (2025):
   - Legal Notice dated 10th January 2025 was sent to both defendants
     demanding cancellation of the fraudulent sale deed and restoration
     of possession.
   - Defendants sent a reply dated 25th January 2025 denying all allegations.
   - Negotiations failed, leading to filing of this suit.

================================================================================
                           PLAINTIFF'S CLAIMS
================================================================================

1. The Sale Deed dated 15th March 2020 is void ab initio as it was executed
   without authority, the GPA being limited in scope and not authorizing sale.

2. The transaction is vitiated by fraud as:
   a) No consideration was actually paid to the Plaintiff
   b) The stated consideration of Rs. 50 Lakhs is grossly inadequate
   c) The Plaintiff never intended to sell the property
   d) Defendant No. 1 was in a fiduciary position and breached trust

3. The Plaintiff has suffered:
   a) Loss of property valued at Rs. 2.5 Crores
   b) Mental agony and harassment
   c) Legal expenses
